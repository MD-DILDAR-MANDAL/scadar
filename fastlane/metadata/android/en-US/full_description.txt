Scadar is an intuitive, completely open-source personal expense and finance tracker designed for privacy and data sovereignty.

Features:
* Income and expense tracking with custom categories
* Monthly budgeting with goal tracking and visual progress indicators
* Recurring transaction management with automatic interval processing
* Multi-currency support and currency conversion
* Financial charts and spending analytics
* 100% offline local storage powered by SQLite
* Seamless local JSON backup & restore without proprietary cloud dependencies
* CSV export for spreadsheets
* Zero ads, zero trackers, and zero telemetry
